# Coffee-Sales-Analysis-using-Python

Coffee Sales Analysis dataset contains detailed records of coffee sales from a vending machine.
It is intended for analysis of purchasing patterns, sales trends, and customer preferences related to coffee products.
The dataset includes column names like date, datetime, cash_type, card, money, coffee_name, month, day, hour.

# Tasks
● TimeSeries Exploratory Data Analysis
● Nextday/week/month sales
● Specific customer purchases

Questions used for visualization and analysis:
1. What is the distribution of coffee sales by type?
2. What is the average money spent for each coffee type?
3. How does payment method distribution look?
4. How do total sales compare across different dates?
5. What are the top 5 most purchased coffees?
6. What is the total revenue generated by each coffee type?
7. How many sales were made for cash vs. card?
8. What are the monthly sales for each coffee?
9. What are the weekday sales for each coffee?
10. What are the total sales by hour of the day?

Requirements: Python Google Colab

Data Files: The analysis is based on vending machine coffee sales data which should be in a CSV or Excel format.

# Conclusion:
From the analysis above, we have uncovered valuable insights into customer shopping patterns on a daily and weekly basis. I have identified the most popular coffee products and observed the shopping trends over time. These findings are instrumental in optimizing inventory planning, designing the layout of vending machines, and determining the ideal restock times for coffee products
